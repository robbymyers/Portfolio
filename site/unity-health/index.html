Unity Health

Brand & Campaign Design — Healthcare System

Design Branding Illustration 3D Campaign

Project Overview

I produced a range of brand and campaign work for Unity Health, a regional healthcare system, translating a single identity across community outreach, environmental design, and digital fundraising.

The "Together We Are CommUnity" campaign paired warm, real patient photography with a hand-lettered logotype to celebrate the people Unity Health serves — neighbors, physicians, and community members alike.

Beyond the campaign, I illustrated and painted a "We're Over the Moon" mural for the children's wing, and modeled a 3D Virtual Shopping Mall for the auxiliary's annual fundraiser — giving staff an interactive, navigable storefront to support the cause.
