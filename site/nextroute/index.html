Overview

Designed to belong

Next Route is a third-party trail navigation app that lives inside Toyota's Audio Multimedia system — and reads as part of it. The Tacoma's 14" display, 12.3" digital cluster, and physical knobs are the hardware constraints we design within, and the host system's visual language is one of them.

Next Route earns its place by behaving like it belongs there, not by announcing itself. The distinctiveness is in what it does at the trailhead.
