## Hero / Intro

**Summer Days** — a recurring seasonal campaign for Shadrachs Coffee

Every summer, Shadrachs runs a Summer Days promotion built around a rotating weekly structure — Triple Points Tuesdays, Scratch-Off Fridays, Sticker Saturdays — and I create the full campaign identity and collateral that carries it.

## Background & Objective

Every summer, Shadrachs runs a Summer Days promotion built around a rotating weekly structure: Triple Points Tuesdays (3x rewards points per drink), Scratch-Off Fridays (a scratch card with every large/XL purchase), and Sticker Saturdays (a free limited-edition sticker with purchase, every Saturday in June and July). The job is the full campaign identity and every piece of collateral that carries it, in-store and out.

## Role & Contributions

Concept and execution both. I present the overall creative direction to my marketing manager and the owner; once it's approved, I own production end to end — from the initial lockup through every deliverable.

## Key Decisions & Creative Rationale

Two decisions shaped the whole campaign. First: lead with motion. Animated reels and posts get made first so the campaign doesn't just scroll past as another static image in someone's feed — motion buys attention a still graphic can't. Second: build it as one system, not a pile of separate assets. Shadrachs owns a billboard in Jonesboro, and the billboard, flyers, cards, and stickers are all built off the same visual system, so the campaign reads as one thing no matter where someone runs into it — feed, drive-thru window, or driving past the billboard.

## Process

Start with a style found on Pinterest, build the "Summer Days" wordmark/lockup in that style, then move straight into animation. Whatever the animation settles on — pacing, texture, color behavior — sets the tone for every static piece that follows. Motion drives the static work, not the other way around.

## Challenge

The deadline, mostly. When a specific animation technique isn't one I know yet, I don't burn the clock fighting it — I switch styles or techniques to get the same energy across a different way, rather than let the whole campaign slip.

## Outcome & Impact

Measurable at the drive-thru: noticeably longer lines specifically on Scratch-Off Fridays and Sticker Saturdays, the two mechanics built around a physical, collectible reward.
