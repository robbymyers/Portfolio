Background & Objective

A summer built on a weekly rhythm

Every summer, Shadrachs runs a Summer Days promotion on a rotating weekly structure. The job is the full campaign identity and every piece of collateral that carries it, in-store and out.

Tuesdays
Triple Points
3× rewards points on every drink. 10 points = 1 free drink.
Fridays
Scratch-Off
A scratch card with every large or XL drink purchased.
Saturdays
Sticker Day
A free limited-edition sticker with purchase, every Saturday in June and July.

Key Decisions & Creative Rationale

Two decisions shaped everything

Decision 01
Lead with motion
Animated reels and posts get made first, so the campaign doesn't just scroll past as another static image in someone's feed. Motion buys attention a still graphic can't.
Decision 02
One system, not a pile of assets
Shadrachs owns a billboard in Jonesboro — and the billboard, flyers, cards, and stickers are all built off the same visual system, so the campaign reads as one thing wherever someone runs into it: feed, drive-thru window, or driving past.

Process

Motion drives the static work

Start with a style found on Pinterest, build the "Summer Days" wordmark and lockup in that style, then move straight into animation.

Whatever the animation settles on — pacing, texture, color behavior — sets the tone for every static piece that follows. Motion drives the static work, not the other way around.

The Challenge

The deadline, mostly. When a specific animation technique isn't one I know yet, I don't burn the clock fighting it — I switch styles or techniques to get the same energy across a different way, rather than let the whole campaign slip.

Outcome & Impact

Measurable at the drive-thru

Noticeably longer lines specifically on Scratch-Off Fridays and Sticker Saturdays — the two mechanics built around a physical, collectible reward.
