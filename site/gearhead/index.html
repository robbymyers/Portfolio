Role Creative Director, Gearhead Outfitters

The 2-minute brand film

Background & Objective

An outdoor store for the whole day

Gearhead is known as an outdoor retailer, but its customers bring it into much more of their lives than the trail. Nurses buy shoes there for twelve-hour shifts. People dress there for a night out. The line had to stretch from a hiking trail to a hospital hallway to downtown without feeling like three ads stitched together.

Wherever life takes you, Go. Whether you're out on a hike with friends, walking long hours on a shift, or out on the town, Gearhead has you covered.

Role & Contributions

Owned how it looks and how it feels

Tone & Feel

Set the emotional direction for the film. Hunter West wrote, directed, and edited it; the job was making sure every cut felt like Gearhead.

Color Direction

Directed the colorist to the final grade, defining the look that ties a trail, a shift, and a night out into one brand.

Key Decisions

Lived in, not catalog

  • A muted, earthy grade with deep blacks and natural skin tones, so it reads as a real day instead of a staged shoot
  • The product rides along: a bottle carried at someone's side, not posed on a rock
  • Candid moments carry the brand, the laugh and the jump into the swimming hole, more than any product shot could
