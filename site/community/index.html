Background & Objective

A campaign that feels like the region it serves

Unity Health wanted a brand campaign that felt like the region it serves — not typical "trust us with your care" hospital marketing. The idea: everyone in the Unity Health network, from physicians to patients, is also just a neighbor. That shared identity is what makes the care personal.

We are volunteers, neighbors, patients, caregivers and teammates. We are doctors, coaches, teachers and friends. Unity Health helps shape the close-knit neighborhoods we live in, as well as the vast network of care across the region. CommUnity doesn't happen alone — it happens by being together.

Key Decisions & Creative Rationale

You can't have community without unity

The core idea is made literal in the wordmark itself — "Unity" pulled out in weight and color inside "CommUnity."

To make the "we're the same community" idea land, nobody is shot in a white coat or an exam room. Every hero image shows someone in their actual life — and physicians and patients get identical visual and verbal treatment on purpose. That's the whole point of the campaign.

The Series

Same treatment, on purpose

Each person is billed by who they are before what they are to the hospital — Mother, then Cardiologist, then Baker, then Searcy Resident. Physicians and patients, shot and framed identically.

Campaign Spot

The :30 spot

The same idea in motion — neighbors who happen to be the people caring for you.

Process

From a format to a system

The hero-image-with-type-over-it format was the starting direction. From there: rounds of review with the marketing coordinators and the marketing leader, then formal brand standards — typography, image treatment, and a layout system.

With the standards in place, the broader team could keep producing consistent ads without design review on every single piece — from portrait ads to banners to merch.

The Challenge

Leadership was ready to send the campaign to a local agency to execute. Before that was final, the logo and a handful of mock ads were built and presented as an alternative — and the marketing leader chose that direction instead.

Outcome & Impact

A marketing line that became literally true

The campaign launched at the end of 2019. When COVID hit the following year, its entire premise — that Unity Health's physicians are literally your neighbors — stopped being a marketing line and became visibly true to the whole community in real time, as those same physicians were on the front lines caring for the people around them.
