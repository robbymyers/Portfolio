## Hero / Intro

**Together We Are CommUnity** — a brand campaign for Unity Health

We are volunteers, neighbors, patients, caregivers and teammates. We are doctors, coaches, teachers and friends. Unity Health helps shape the close-knit neighborhoods we live in, as well as the vast network of care across the region. That connection is what forms the bond between patient and provider that's unique to the place we call home. CommUnity doesn't happen alone — it happens by being together.

## Background & Objective

Unity Health wanted a brand campaign that felt like the region it serves, not like typical "trust us with your care" hospital marketing. The idea: everyone in the Unity Health network, from physicians to patients, is also just a neighbor — and that shared identity is what makes the care personal.

## Role & Contributions

Owned the creative end to end: developed the logo, set the messaging direction, art directed and produced every ad in the series, and wrote the brand standards that governed how the campaign was used across the organization's marketing team.

## Key Decisions & Creative Rationale

The core idea — you can't have community without unity — is made literal in the wordmark itself, with "Unity" pulled out in weight and color inside "CommUnity."

To make the "we're the same community" idea land, nobody is shot in a white coat or an exam room. Every hero image shows someone in their actual life:

- **Dr. Katherine Durham**, a Unity Health cardiologist, photographed at home with her daughter — billed first as "Mother," then "Unity Health Cardiologist," then "Baker," then "Searcy Resident"
- **Steven Bowen**, a veteran and total joint replacement patient, photographed outside his home next to his car — billed as "Veteran," "Total Joint Replacement Patient," "Car Enthusiast," "Friend"

Physicians and patients get identical visual and verbal treatment on purpose — that's the whole point of the campaign.

## Process

The hero-image-with-type-over-it format was the starting direction. From there: rounds of review with the marketing coordinators and the marketing leader, then formal brand standards (typography, image treatment, layout system) so the broader team could keep producing consistent ads without design review on every single piece.

## Challenge

Marketing leadership was ready to send the campaign to a local agency to execute the vision, styling, and messaging. Before that decision was final, the logo and a handful of mock ads were built and presented as an alternative — and the marketing leader chose that direction instead.

## Outcome & Impact

The campaign launched at the end of 2019. When COVID hit the following year, its entire premise — that Unity Health's physicians are literally your neighbors — stopped being a marketing line and became visibly true to the whole community in real time, as those same physicians were on the front lines caring for the people around them.
